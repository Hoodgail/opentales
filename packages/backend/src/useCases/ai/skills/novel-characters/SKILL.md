# Novel Characters

Read the author's goal, current manuscript, character profiles, and planning docs. Develop the ensemble as the story needs it.

- Give major characters conflicting wants, needs, beliefs, secrets, knowledge boundaries, voice cues, and arc pressure.
- Differentiate characters through relationships: each important belief or coping strategy should put pressure on somebody else.
- Keep spoilers and private motivations out of reader-facing summaries.
- Create or edit character profiles and relationships with the project tools. Keep deeper notes, open questions, and progress in project docs.
- Re-read existing profiles before editing and verify tool receipts before claiming success.
